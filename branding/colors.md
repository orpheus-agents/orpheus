# Orpheus colours

| Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0E0E10` | Wordmark on light backgrounds, background of icons and exports |
| Paper | `#EDEDEB` | Wordmark on dark backgrounds |

Ink and Paper are the AgentBox neutrals.

## Mark gradients

Each ribbon has a linear gradient with three stops. Gradient lines are in
[`scripts/build.py`](scripts/build.py).

| Ribbon | Start | Middle | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top-left | `#007B91` | `#09BBB4` | `#3FF1B6` |
| Right | `#03B399` | `#27DDA2` | `#43F59F` |
| Bottom | `#027281` | `#029B9D` | `#05BFAE` |
| Lower-left | `#008795` | `#06A8AB` | `#3AD2BC` |
